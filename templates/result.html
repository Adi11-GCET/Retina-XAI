Patient Preparation Checklist Before Visiting Specialist:
  • Bring this printed AI screening report and any recent blood glucose / HbA1c lab records.
  • Have an escort or family member accompany you, as pupil-dilating eye drops temporarily cause blurry vision for 3-4 hours.
  • Bring UV sunglasses or a hat to wear after dilated pupil examination.
